Ikat, from thread to textile

Designed before
it is woven.

Unlike printed cloth, an Ikat pattern is imagined directly onto yarn. Portions of thread are tightly bound, dyed and carefully opened before weaving begins. The resulting softness at every edge is not an imperfection—it is the signature of the maker’s hand.
